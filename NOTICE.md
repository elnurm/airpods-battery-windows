The battery parser was implemented using publicly documented Apple BLE proximity-pairing fields, notably [stuffz/airpods](https://github.com/stuffz/airpods), its [BLE notes](https://github.com/stuffz/airpods/blob/main/docs/ble.md), and [model/field reference](https://github.com/stuffz/airpods/blob/main/src/aap/proximity.hpp). Those notes also credit earlier community reverse-engineering work. No upstream AirPods application binaries or icon packs are bundled.
